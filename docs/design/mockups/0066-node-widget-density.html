A study of density in the app's NodeRow and NodeList widgets: width-aware tag packing, a three-step row-height ladder using the app's curved inset kind-stripe, and the decided tree-hierarchy fix.

NodeRow · NodeList — ADR-0066

Node Widget Density Study

Everything reduces to a NodeRow in a NodeList. The app shipped the Editorial Card — calm, roomy, serif headers — which reads beautifully on a flat lore list and pays too much in density and hierarchy where it's forced into dense, nested, or picker contexts.

Decided

  • ✓Width-aware pill packing, full-width tag line.
  • ✓Comfortable · Compact · Dense — the NodeList picks its step.
  • ✓Tree fix = typographic + indent + child tint.
  • ✓Density is a NodeList mode.

The stripe — settled

  • ✓The app's curved inset stripe — the band clipped by the row's rounded corners — at every density.
  • ✓A dense row is just a tighter rounded card; the same stripe curves along its corners.
  • ✓No capsules, no square bars, no horizontal dividers.

02

The density ladder

Decided

Same six lore entries at three densities — a NodeList picks its step. A flat lore list stays comfortable; a deep tree or a picker goes tighter. Tags drop out at the dense end; the stripe carries the kind.

The stripe rule, one for every density: a member row carries the app's curved inset stripe in its kind's colour — the band the row's rounded corners clip so it curves top and bottom. A group header (the frame) carries none. A dense row is just a tighter rounded card, so the very same stripe curves along its corners — no capsules, no square bars.

03

The tree reads upside-down

Decided

Container was --fs-md serif (13px) under --fs-lg leaves (15px). The fix: header steps up to --fs-xl serif with a count, leaves recede to --fs-md, and children get indent + a stronger tint (tiers widened so they read in dark).
