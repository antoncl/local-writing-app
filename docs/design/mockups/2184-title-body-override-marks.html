Title and body override marks

A book overrides an inherited entry's title or body the way it already overrides a field. Neither has a rail row, so the existing ti-versions mark moves to the thing it marks: after the title, and on the Body tab. Same tint, same hover chip, same wording as the rail's rank row on the right.

This can't be undone.

What each mark does

  • Title mark: hover or focus shows “↩ Reset to Aetheria”. Clicking resets at once, like a rail field; the old title is one retype away.
  • Body mark: the chip ends in “…” because the click asks first. Resetting throws away prose the author wrote, and removing the override isn't snapshotted.
  • Tooltips name the canon: the title's names Aetheria's title; the body's says the text is the book's.
  • Keyboard: each mark is a button and a tab stop, so the reset is never hover-only (#517's rule).

What it deliberately doesn't do

  • No banner, no label, no tinted title or body text. Inherited is the default and reads quiet; only the mark says "overridden".
  • No new glyph and no new colour: the mark and --star are the rail's.
  • No mark at all when "Editing at" is Aetheria: that view is the canon.
  • Prompts get nothing: they change title and body only by cloning (§5).
